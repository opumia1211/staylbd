Place official platform logos here to show in the footer "Get our app" section.
When admin does NOT upload a custom image and selects platform (Android, iOS, Windows, Mac, Desktop),
the system will look for these files (first match used):

  android.png or android.svg or android.webp
  ios.png or ios.svg or ios.webp
  windows.png or windows.svg or windows.webp
  mac.png or mac.svg or mac.webp
  desktop.png or desktop.svg or desktop.webp

If no file is found, a built-in icon is shown instead.
Recommended size: 44x44 px or similar square. Format: PNG or SVG.
